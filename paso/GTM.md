# Paso — go-to-market plan

**Status:** draft plan, October 2026. Paso is live with one customer. Everything below that is not yet true is labelled as a plan or a hypothesis. There are no customer counts, prices or testimonials yet, and none are invented here.

## 1. Where we are

- **Product:** live at [holapaso.com](https://holapaso.com). Workflows, jobs, private client links, photo-to-PDF, bilingual UI, WhatsApp notifications and reminders, push notifications, amounts and money summaries, and an MCP connector so Paso can be run from Claude.
- **First customer:** a family import/export business with staff in Chile and Mexico, working mostly on phones.
- **Built to be market-neutral:** no industry-specific words, defaults or rules. Labels, units and currencies are set by each business.

## 2. Who it's for

**Beachhead (now):** small import/export and customs-brokerage teams in Mexico and Chile that already run client paperwork over WhatsApp. This is the first customer's world, so the workflows, the vocabulary problems and the WhatsApp habits are known first-hand.

**Next (hypothesis to test):** any small service team whose clients have to send documents in steps. The brand boards already sketch four:

| Trade | Who buys | The paperwork |
|---|---|---|
| Import / customs | Customs brokers | Invoices, packing lists, permits, duties |
| Tax returns | Tax preparers | Slips, IDs, signed forms |
| Home closings | Real estate offices | Contracts, proofs of funds, inspections |
| Visa applications | Immigration services | Passports, forms, appointments |

**Who uses it, and who decides:** the owner of a small team decides (team size is a hypothesis to test with design partners); the staff use it every day; the clients never install anything.

## 3. Positioning

> For small teams that chase client documents over WhatsApp, Paso turns each job into one shared map: clients see what's needed and send it from one link, staff see what's waiting on whom. Unlike generic form or project tools, the client needs no account, and the business keeps talking to clients on WhatsApp, where they already are.

**Messages, by audience:**

- **Owner:** "Stop chasing documents by phone. See every job's next step at a glance."
- **Staff:** "Photos arrive as PDFs, filed to the right step. Send back with a reason in one tap."
- **Client (in the link preview):** "Sus documentos. Necesitamos 1 documento." Plain words, the business's name first, Paso small underneath.

**Brand rule that shapes GTM:** *the business leads, Paso supports.* Clients trust the people they already work with, so the business's name goes first on every page and message. This makes every client link a quiet, credible ad for Paso ("con Paso").

## 4. Channels

| Channel | Why it fits | First move |
|---|---|---|
| Founder-led sales through the first customer's network | Brokers, forwarders and agents already know each other | Ask the first customer for three introductions |
| The client link itself | Every job sends dozens of clients a Paso-branded page | Add a "Made with Paso" link to the done screen (hypothesis: low-cost referral loop) |
| Trade associations and WhatsApp groups of customs brokers (Mexico, Chile) | Where the beachhead already talks | One demo per association, in Spanish |
| Workflow templates | Show value before setup ("Mexico import", "US export", "Air freight") | Ship ready-made templates per trade |
| Claude / MCP | Paso can be run from Claude, which is new for this market | A short demo video for AI-curious owners |

## 5. Launch phases

| Phase | Goal | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **0. Prove it with one** (now) | The first customer runs all new jobs in Paso | Every new job runs in Paso for a month; no documents lost in chat |
| **1. Five design partners** | Same trade, other businesses, set up in under an hour | 5 teams active weekly; setup with no help from me |
| **2. Second trade** | Prove the market-neutral claim with tax preparers or real estate | One trade outside logistics runs a full cycle |
| **3. Self-serve** | Sign-up, templates, billing | Teams start without a call |

## 6. Pricing (open question)

No pricing exists yet. Hypotheses to test with design partners:

- **Per active job** (fits seasonal trades like tax returns) vs **per staff seat** (fits steady teams like brokers).
- A free tier capped by active jobs, so small teams can start without a sales call.
- Clients are always free and never need an account.

## 7. How we'll measure it

| Stage | Metric |
|---|---|
| Activation | Time from sign-up to the first client link sent |
| Value | Share of client steps completed from the link without staff help |
| Retention | Weekly active teams; jobs started per team per week |
| Proof of the problem | Reminders sent by hand per job (should fall toward zero) |
| Referral | New teams arriving from a client link or an introduction |

## 8. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp policy or pricing changes | Links work without the API; WhatsApp is a channel, not the product |
| "Market-neutral" turns generic | Win one trade first; templates carry the trade-specific language |
| Trademark | US and Mexico clearance recommended before marketing spend |
| Owners don't want "another tool" | Clients install nothing; staff keep WhatsApp; the first win is fewer phone calls |
